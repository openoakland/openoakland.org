- **Where:** Oakland City Hall, Hearing Room 3, 1 Frank H Ogawa Plaza
- **Cost:** Free, lunch provided

CityCamp Oakland is back. It's a participant-led unconference where anyone can propose, lead, or join sessions about the issues and opportunities shaping life in Oakland.

## How an unconference works

The schedule starts empty. Attendees propose sessions on the day and decide together what gets discussed, on topics like:

- Public transit
- Housing
- Parks
- Education
- Open data
- Community safety
- Climate resilience
- Local arts

Sessions take whatever form suits them, whether that's a discussion, a workshop, a demo, or a problem-solving lab.

The format is deliberately open, so the agenda reflects what people who live and work here actually care about. Residents, city staff, students, technologists, and community organizers are all welcome.
