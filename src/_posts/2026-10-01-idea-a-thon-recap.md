On Tuesday, September 29, OpenOakland and Northeastern University Oakland co-hosted [A Beautiful Oakland: A Civic Tech Idea-A-Thon](/updates/a-beautiful-oakland-idea-a-thon/) as part of [Oakland Tech Week](https://oaklandtechweek.com/). Residents, students, technologists, and city leaders spent the afternoon at Northeastern's Oakland campus generating ideas for tackling illegal dumping in Oakland.

## Who we heard from

The afternoon opened with a panel of people working on illegal dumping every day:

- **Kristin Hathaway**, Assistant Director, Oakland Public Works
- **John Brockett**, Faith in Action East Bay

Panelist **Brian Johnson** of Aerbits was unable to join us due to a family emergency.

We were also grateful for remarks from **Councilmember Kevin Jenkins**, **Councilmember Rowena Brown**, and **Councilmember Ken Houston**.

## Idea highlights

![Idea-A-Thon attendees talking around a table covered in worksheets and Panel Notes cards, with other groups working in the background](/assets/images/blog/idea-a-thon-recap-table.jpg)

Working in small groups, attendees brought creative, neighborhood-rooted ideas, including:

- **Scheduled bulky pickup**, so residents can plan ahead instead of waiting on a request
- **A sticker campaign** to raise awareness of the City's existing bulky pickup service
- **Beautifying existing dump spots** to discourage repeat dumping
- **Engaging youth** as community advocates, and through paid employment opportunities

…and many more. Many of these ideas are exactly where civic tech can help, from making city services easier to find and use to helping residents and city staff share information about what's happening on their blocks.

![One group's idea canvas for the problem statement "How might we make legal disposal easier and more appealing than dumping?", with sticky notes clustered into themes like making existing service better, targeted education, beautification, lower cost, corporate responsibility, and enforcement](/assets/images/blog/idea-a-thon-recap-prevent-canvas.jpg)

## What's next

We're compiling the ideas generated at the Idea-A-Thon and will share them with the Oakland City Council and with Northeastern University faculty and researchers.
