See how Oakland volunteers are using AI to solve real civic problems, meet the community building these solutions, and learn how you can join or get their support.

This is one of two OpenOakland events we are hosting as part of [Oakland Tech Week](https://oaklandtechweek.com/). Earlier that afternoon, join us for [A Beautiful Oakland: A Civic Tech Idea-A-Thon](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/a-beautiful-oakland-a-civic-tech-idea-a-thon-tickets-1996656041970?aff=openoaklandwebsite).

- **When:** Tuesday, September 29, 6:30-8:00pm
- **Where:** Oakland City Hall, Hearing Room 3, 1 Frank H Ogawa Plaza, Oakland

## What to expect

- **Two to four short project demos** showcasing OpenOakland projects that incorporate AI tools, with remarks from the volunteers who built them and the community stakeholders they serve
- **A structured networking session** afterward

Bring your own community-oriented project idea and get free support from our volunteer teams. That is what we are here for.
