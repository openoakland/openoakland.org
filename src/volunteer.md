Interested in contributing to civic tech in Oakland? We welcome people of all skill levels and backgrounds — technical skills are not required.

## How to get involved

### 1. Join us on Slack
Our Slack workspace is where the community connects, collaborates, and coordinates. [Join OpenOakland on Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/openoakland/shared_invite/zt-3yesrb28g-A8Wv1kcLjBwBDJlT3G_xVQ) and introduce yourself in [**#oo-meet-and-greet**](https://openoakland.slack.com/messages/CGEE2C76E).

### 2. Attend our Monthly Meeting
We meet on the first Tuesday of every month at 6:30pm at Oakland City Hall (Hearing Room 3). It's a great way to meet the community, learn about active projects, and find where you can contribute. [RSVP on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/OpenOakland/events/).

### 3. Explore our projects
Browse our [active projects](/projects/) to find one that matches your interests and skills. Each project card includes a Slack channel where you can connect directly with the project lead.

### 4. Get in touch
Have questions or not sure where to start? Email us at [steering@openoakland.org](mailto:steering@openoakland.org) and we'll help you find the right fit.

## Community standards

OpenOakland is committed to creating a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment for everyone.

- **[Code of Conduct](/code-of-conduct):** All participants are expected to uphold our community standards, which value diverse identities and contributions.
- **Anti-Harassment Policy:** We prohibit harassment of any kind. If you experience or witness harassment, please contact us at [steering@openoakland.org](mailto:steering@openoakland.org).
