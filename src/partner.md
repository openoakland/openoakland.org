OpenOakland connects public agencies and nonprofits with skilled tech volunteers to increase their community impact through the use of technology. If you're facing challenges in delivering services to Oakland or East Bay residents, we can help - at no cost.

[Partner with Us](#apply-now){: .btn .btn-primary }

## Eligibility Criteria & Expectations

* Non-profits or public agencies serving Oakland or East Bay residents
* Demonstrated need of support
* Project can be completed within 3-6 months
* Technology-related problem or potential for a tech solution
* Dedicated point of contact prepared to spend 2-3 hours a week interacting with the project team

Sound like a good fit? Unsure? Apply and we can figure it out together.

[Apply now!](#apply-now){: .btn .btn-primary }

## Process

Once you apply, we will review your submission as soon as possible and invite you for a consultation to learn more about your project scope and challenges. Expect us to reach out to you shortly after to inform you of our decision.

## FAQ

### What types of organizations do you work with?
We primarily serve Oakland-based public agencies and nonprofit organizations. Organizations serving other residents in other parts of the East Bay may also be a good fit. Some of our past partnerships include: Oakland's Public Ethics Commission, Oakland Public Works / Department of Transportation, [Oakland Tech Exchange](https://www.techexchange.org/), and La Escuelita Elementary School.

### How long do engagements typically last?
Most projects are completed within 3-6 months, though timing varies based on scope and complexity. We may suggest breaking down a problem into small pieces and follow-up engagements rather than having one long running project.

### What does it cost?
Our services are provided for free by skilled volunteers. We ask only for your time and commitment to the project's success.
